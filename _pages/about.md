I am a Ph.D. student at **Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU)** and **Shanghai Innovation Institute(SII)**, majoring in Control Science and Engineering with a focus on **AI Infrastructure**. My research lies at the intersection of large-scale network optimization and intelligent power systems.

* **2025-07-14** – **A Fast Solver-Free Algorithm for Traffic Engineering in Large-Scale Data Center Network** accepted to **NSDI'26 (23rd USENIX NSDI, Seattle, May 4–6, 2026)**. Acceptance: 50/207 (24.2%). (First author)

## Research Interests

* 🔍 **Data Center Networks (DCN):** Traffic Engineering (TE) and Topology Engineering in large-scale and reconfigurable networks.
* 📊 **Microgrid Management:** Optimal scheduling and planning for microgrids with high-penetration renewable energy.
* 💡 **Power System Analysis:** Linear power flow modeling and data generation considering spatial correlations.
