Published in **NSDI '26** (23rd USENIX NSDI, Seattle, May 4–6, 2026). This work proposes Geminet, a duality-based, topology-agnostic update operator that delivers lightweight, fast traffic engineering across changing topologies.

**Authors:** Ximeng Liu; Zhuoran Liu; **Yingming Mao**; Yatao Li; Shizhen Zhao; Xinbing Wang.
