This work treats the timing of high-power service as a planning-stage flexibility resource. It co-optimizes photovoltaic, wind, battery, fuel-cell, and hydrogen-storage capacities with a contiguous daily service window while guaranteeing a two-tier service envelope under renewable-availability uncertainty.

**Authors:** **Yingming Mao**; Yuzhou Zhou; Qiaozhu Zhai; Jiexing Zhao; Xiaoyu Cao; Lei Wu; Xiaohong Guan.
